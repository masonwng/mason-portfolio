    how to play

    Guess a finished Summoner’s Rift item from its direct recipe components. There is no guess limit. Each wrong guess reveals another component. The total gold cost appears after two misses, and item categories after three.

    For each guess, the arrow tells you whether the answer costs more or less gold. The component count tells you how many direct ingredients your guess shares with the answer, including duplicates.

    Your puzzle score starts at 6/6 for one guess and drops by one point per additional guess, to 0/6 after six misses. Ranks: 1 Challenger, 2 Grandmaster, 3 Master, 4 Diamond, 5 Emerald, 6 Platinum, 7 Gold, 8 Silver, 9 Bronze, 10+ Iron. This is just a game score, not your real League rank.

    Daily uses the same item for everyone and resets at midnight UTC. Practice is unlimited. Progress stays in this browser. No login or live game connection.
