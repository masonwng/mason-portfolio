    How it works

    One correct answer unlocks the next grade. Standard lets you retry and learn from hints. Showing a solution means you need a fresh question at that grade before advancing.

    One-Life Challenge ends on the first incorrect answer. Hints are available and recorded; solutions appear after the run. Invalid input formats do not cost a life. Fastest records require a complete run without hints.

    Time Attack gives you 60 seconds. Every two correct answers moves you up a grade (up to Grade 9). Points are 10 × the grade level, multiplied by your combo: ×2 after 3 in a row, up to ×5. A wrong answer or skip resets the combo. Leaving the tab ends the round. Your best score is saved in this browser.

    Fractions and decimals are accepted. For algebra, use x, parentheses, +, −, *, / and ^. For example, 2(x+1) and 2x+2 are equivalent. Put + C at the end of an indefinite integral. More complex notation uses choices or numerical answers.

    Progress saves in this browser. The timer pauses while this tab is hidden. Changing mode starts a new run. These are sampled topic challenges, not a formal grade assessment.
